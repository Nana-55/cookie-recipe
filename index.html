Homemade Cookie Recipe

Here is my favorite recipe to make soft and delicious cookies at home.

Tried this recipe? Leave your feedback at the rating section below.

Recipe Overview

Freezing the dough before baking is very important so cookies hold shape.

Ingredients

  • 100g brown sugar & 60g white sugar
  • 100g butter
  • 1 whole egg and 1 egg yolk
  • 1 tsp vanilla & pinch of salt
  • 230g flour, 1 tsp baking powder, 1/2 tsp baking soda

How to Bake

  1. Mix softened butter with sugars.
  2. Add egg, yolk, and vanilla, then mix.
  3. Add dry ingredients and mix.
  4. Freeze dough for at least 1 hour.
  5. Bake bottom 9 mins, top broil 2 mins.
